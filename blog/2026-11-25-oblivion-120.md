`1.2.0` is out, and the biggest change is that the server keeps the time: everyone on it is in the same hour of the same day. Bans, kicks and whitelists hold where they used to let people through. Your level and your skills survive a logout, and poison stays on the weapon you put it on.

There is nothing to install if you play. The launcher updates your game when you join a server that has moved to `1.2.0`, and until a server updates you stay on `1.1.1` there.

Mods need rebuilding, and most need editing, so read [Upgrading](/releases/oblivionmp-sdk-120#upgrading) before you start.

## What's new

### One clock for the server

The server keeps the time of day and the date, and every player's game follows it, so you no longer walk through a door into a different hour.

Waiting and sleeping are off as a result, since one player's six-hour nap would be everyone's six hours. The wait key, `T` by default, does nothing. A bed opens the level-up menu if you have a level-up ready, and otherwise does nothing. A server owner who wants them back turns `AllowTimeSkip` on in `mods/OblivionMp.Sdk/server/config.json` and `HideWaitMenu` off in `mods/OblivionMp.Sdk/client/config.json`, which the server hands out to players.

### Connections, bans and whitelists

A banned or kicked player stays out, and so does one who is not on the whitelist. A handful of ways around them are closed. One account also plays from one PC at a time: a second machine no longer takes over a session that is already running.

A dropped connection keeps retrying now, where the game used to try once and give up. It stops when you quit, or when the server gives a reason that no retry will fix, such as a kick or a ban.

## Fixed

* Logging out no longer costs you the level and skill progress you made since your last level-up.
* A fortify effect from a spell or potion wears off when it should. Quitting while one was running used to make it part of your character for good.
* Poison belongs to the weapon now, not to your character, so a poisoned blade you trade, store or drop stays poisoned. Poison a second weapon and both keep their own.
* Looting a dead archer gives you their whole stack of arrows, instead of one arrow per pickup.
* A ring on an NPC's left hand comes off again, instead of staying worn.
* Loading screens show your name and level instead of “level 0”. The one at startup shows no player line, since your character is not known until the server sends it.
* An NPC killed in the middle of a swing no longer repeats that swing for as long as the body lies there.
* Summons no longer stay behind after a disconnect or a reload.
* The `Rebirth` command works without a quicksave. It used to press the game's quick-load, which does nothing unless you had made one.

## Upgrading {#upgrading}

### Your mods

Bump the SDK packages and your manifest's `minimumVersion` to `1.2.0`, then work through this:

| Was, in `1.1.1` | Now, in `1.2.0` |
|---|---|
| `SkillAttributes`, read and write | `SkillAttributes` is read-only and holds the effective values, base plus running effects. Write `BaseSkillAttributes` instead. |
| The `Time` mixin, on `Area`, owner-based | Renamed `GameTime`, because `Time` inside a service is the service's update clock. It is on `World` now and server-authoritative, so a client cannot write it. |
| `Time.GameHour`, a float from 0 to 24 | `GameTime.TimeOfDay`, a read-only `TimeSpan`, and `GameTime.TimeOfDayMinutes`, a float counting from midnight |
| `Time.GameDaysPassed`, a float | `GameTime.GameDay`, a `uint`, still counting days since the world started |
| `Time.TimeScale` | `GameTime.Timescale`, lower-case `s` |
| `SessionClock` | `WorldClock`, in `OblivionMP.SDK.Serverside.Systems`. `NowGameHours()` works the same and is no longer per area. |
| `TimeChangeEvent` | `TimeSkipRequestEvent`, raised when a player confirms the wait menu. It is a request rather than a notification, so read `GameTime` to follow the clock itself. |
| `Equipment.CurrentPoison`, one per character | `ItemExtraData.Poison`, one per item, on inventory entries and dropped items |

Also new: `LevelMixin.LevelExperience` for progress towards the next level, `GameTime.Date` and the `OblivionMP.SDK.Common.Calendar` namespace for the Tamrielic calendar, and `ServerConfig` and `ClientConfig` for the SDK's own settings.

`WorldClock` is a service like any other, so a mod that wants to run time its own way disables it through `IServices` and advances `GameTime` itself. Time skipping is the same: handle `TimeSkipRequestEvent` and do what you like with it. The SDK applies the skip itself when `AllowTimeSkip` is on, so a handler of your own that also advances the clock skips the time twice. Leave the setting off and do the work yourself. [Time, weather and session](/oblivion-mp/docs/Modding/Game/time-weather-session) covers all of this.

Recompile even if nothing above applies to your mod: one built against `1.1.0` or `1.1.1` will not load on `1.2.0`.

### Your server

1. Recompile your mods against the 1.2.0 SDK.
2. Download the 1.2.0 server package for Windows or Linux from [Installation](/oblivion-mp/docs/Server/installation).
3. Stop the old server.
4. Copy the `saves` folder, the `data` folder, the `branding` folder and the server's own `config.json` from the old server folder into the new one.
5. Copy your own mods into the new `mods` folder, and with them any config file you edited in a mod the package ships.
6. Start the new server.

Existing saves load as they are, skills and all.
