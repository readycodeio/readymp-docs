The OblivionMP server `1.0.1` is out; the SDK stays at `1.0.0`. This release offers improved stability, with fixes on top of `1.0.0`: four crashes in the magic menu, at merchants, in combat and when closing the game, and a merchant's item list that lost its scroll position.

<!-- truncate -->

## Fixed

- Fixed a crash that occurred when a spell or potion effect expired while the magic menu was open.
- Fixed a crash that occurred when two players bought the same item from a merchant at the same time.
- Fixed a crash that occurred when closing the game.
- Fixed a crash that occurred when an NPC's combat target died while another player was nearby.
- Fixed an issue where the merchant's item list kept jumping and losing its scroll position after buying many arrows.

## Updating

* **Servers.** Downloads for both platforms are on the [Installation](/oblivion-mp/docs/Server/installation) page.
* **Mods.** No changes needed. The SDK has not changed since `1.0.0`, so existing mods keep working without recompiling.

Check [Known issues](/oblivion-mp/docs/known-issues) before reporting a bug. Anything not on it, report on the [OblivionMP Discord](https://discord.gg/obmp).
